Our Platform

Immigration

Illegal Immigration is not something we want. We will try to make the immigration process easier so immigrants don’t have to go over the border. We will try to provide classes for immigrants to take, in these classes they will learn what they need to know for the citizenship test, so they will be able to get their citizenship more easily.

Taxes

We wish for taxes to still be a thing in our government, but lowered to around 14% to 32% in order to be fair for everyone. We will not have taxes on overtime pay, and we will reduce taxes for the elders as well.

Education

We want an education that is strong yet purposeful. The education will teach kids more skills that they will be able to use in their adulthood plus the ones they are already learning. There will be only four days per week and because most students do not like school, we will be changing the curriculum of it. For starters, 7th grade to 12th grade, there would be opportunities for jobs that would be beneficial for their future. Jobs that they will be interested in would make them feel less stressed and make school a better place to be at if add these things. We will also continue to provide free lunches to students.

Healthcare

Citizens are offered various amounts of healthcare that can help people with illnesses or disabilities to the furthest and the uttermost extent with the help of medicine and future robotics. We believe that American healthcare should be either free, or extremely decreased in costs. Around 48,000 people die annually due to not being able to afford healthcare or insurance. We will lower the prices of drug store medicine and make sure elders receive better healthcare after retirement. For abortion we will leave it up to each state to decide their policies.

Environment

Pollution needs to be watched more closely and be intervened with, so the Earth improves every year. Also, things like deforestation and overpopulation that hurt our environments need to be helped with programs that allow for people to branch out without destroying forests and our planet. We will try to use more sustainable and less harmful energy sources like solar panels.

Economy

The economy is extremely important for the success of a nation, so we want to have a good economy. We know that inflation is a big problem, but the minimum wage not going up with inflation is an even bigger problem. We want the minimum wage to scale while trying to bring inflation down, this can make our citizens more comfortable in their day to day life.

Foreign Policy

We want to make stronger connections with other countries and maintain peace where necessary. However, if we absolutely have to, we will use our military to protect us. This is only a last-ditch effort, and will only be used if necessary. We believe international imports, and exports should be decreased, this helps make more materials be American-made, and offers more job opportunities for Americans.
