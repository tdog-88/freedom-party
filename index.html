Our Platform

Immigration

Illegal Immigration is not something we want. We will try to make the immigration process easier so immigrants don’t have to go over the border!

Taxes

We want taxes to still exist, but they will be lowered. Taxes will be going mostly to the military.

Education

A strong yet purposeful education where students would learn various amounts of learning that would be helpful in their adult life. School should be later in the morning, but later in the afternoon. We will only be in school for around six months.

Healthcare

Citizens are offered various amounts of healthcare that can help people with illnesses or disabilities to the furthest and the uttermost extent with the help of medicine and future robotics. We believe that American healthcare should be either free, or extremely decreased in costs. Many Americans die every year because they couldn't afford help, we want to stop that.

Environment

Pollution needs to be watched more closely and be intervened with, so the Earth stops getting so much worse every year. Also, things like deforestation and over population that hurt our environments need to be helped with programs that allow for people to branch out without destroying forests and our planet.

Economy

The economy is extremely important for the success of a nation, so we want to have a good economy. We know that inflation is a big problem, but the minimum wage not going up with inflation is an even bigger problem. We want the minimum wage to scale with inflation, this can make our citizens more comfortable.

Foreign Policy

We want to make stronger connections with other countries and maintain peace where necessary. However, if we absolutely have to, we will use our military to protect us. This is only a last-ditch effort, and will only be used if necessary. We believe international imports, and exports should be decreased, this helps make more materials be American-made, and offers more job opportunities for Americans.
